# Imager 1.1.0

_Released 2026-07-31_

This release adds a loupe, borrowed from Aperture.

## Highlights

- **Loupe.**
  View ▸ Show Loupe, or ⌥⌘L, puts a magnifier under the cursor and lets you sweep it across the
  image.
  It shows one image pixel per point, so it is a way of judging focus and sharpness without
  leaving fit-to-window — which is where you spend most of your time.
- **Sized to suit.**
  Settings ▸ General sets how wide it is, from 60 to 400 points across.

## One thing worth knowing

The loupe always shows actual pixels, whatever the view is doing.
So when the image is fitted to the window it magnifies a long way — around eight times on a
36 megapixel photo — and once you have zoomed in past 100% it magnifies *less* than the view.
That is deliberate: it is a window onto the real pixels rather than a fixed multiplier.

## Notes

- Beyond the edge of the image the loupe shows black rather than smearing.
- Its ring stays the same thickness on screen at any zoom.

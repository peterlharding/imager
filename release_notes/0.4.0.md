# Imager 0.4.0

_Released 2026-07-26_

This release adds zoom and pan for inspecting images closely.

## Highlights

- **Zoom and pan.**
  Scroll to zoom (centered on the cursor), drag to pan, and pinch to zoom on a trackpad.
- **Precise zoom controls.**
  Zoom In (⌘=), Zoom Out (⌘-), Zoom to Fit (⌘0), and Actual Size / 100% (⌘1),
  plus toolbar buttons that show the current zoom percentage.
- **Sensible defaults.**
  Images open zoomed to fit the window; small images are shown at 100% rather than being upscaled,
  and are centered when smaller than the window.

## Under the hood

- Image display moved to an AppKit `NSScrollView`-backed view (`ZoomableImageView`) for smooth,
  high-quality magnification, correct high-DPI rendering, and scrollbars.
- 100% maps one image pixel to one point, based on the image's true pixel dimensions.
